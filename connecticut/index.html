Connecticut driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Connecticut. It is written from Connecticut Department of Motor Vehicles pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: Residents aged 16 and older can apply for a learner's permit.
  • First license: The legal age is 16 years old for both a learner's permit and a driver's license.
  • Written test: Pass a vision test and a 25-question written knowledge test to get your permit.
  • Road test: you're ready to take a road test-or "driving test."

Documents

  • Identity document. Most REAL ID applicants need a U.S. passport or U.S. birth certificate. Source (official site)
  • Social Security card. A Social Security card can still be used to verify identity for a REAL ID. Source (official site)
  • Connecticut address proof. Two pieces of mail from different sources, dated within 90 days, show Connecticut residency. Source (official site)
  • Application form R-229. Bring the completed non-commercial learner's permit and license application to the appointment. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Connecticut driver license or permit?

DMV publishes a document list that includes Identity document, Social Security card, Connecticut address proof, Application form R-229. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Connecticut?

A nonresident who is at least 16 may drive in Connecticut with an out-of-state learner's permit, but that permit cannot be transferred into a Connecticut permit.

How do I book the Connecticut road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through DMV, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are Connecticut license and permit fees listed?

DMV publishes these amounts: License examination fee $40; Class D learner's permit $19; New non-commercial driver's license $84. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
