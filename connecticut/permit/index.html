Connecticut learner permit requirements

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

In Connecticut, Connecticut Department of Motor Vehicles publishes Residents aged 16 and older can apply for a learner's permit as the minimum age for a learner permit. Permit age (official site)

Quick answer

  • Minimum age: Residents aged 16 and older can apply for a learner's permit.
  • Holding period: 120 days with school training, or 180 days with home training.
  • Practice hours: 40 hours.
