Connecticut teen driver license rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Teen licensing in Connecticut is the graduated path published by Connecticut Department of Motor Vehicles. Adult applicants can have different rules.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: Residents aged 16 and older can apply for a learner's permit.
  • First license: The legal age is 16 years old for both a learner's permit and a driver's license.
  • Full license: Until the driver's eighteenth birthday , they may not drive between hours of 11 p.m.
  • Practice hours: 40 hours.
  • Night rule: Until the driver's eighteenth birthday , they may not drive between hours of 11 p.m. - 5 a.m.
  • Passengers: First six months: only a parent, legal guardian, driving instructor, or qualified trainer.
