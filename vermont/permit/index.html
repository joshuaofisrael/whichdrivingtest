Vermont learner permit requirements

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

In Vermont, Vermont Department of Motor Vehicles publishes You must be at least 15 years old to take the test. as the minimum age for a learner permit. Permit age (official site)

Quick answer

  • Minimum age: You must be at least 15 years old to take the test.
  • Holding period: at least one (1) year.
  • Practice hours: forty hours of practice behind the wheel, with at least ten hours being nighttime driving.

Knowledge test

If you fail a knowledge test you must wait at least one day before retaking the test. Written test (official site)
