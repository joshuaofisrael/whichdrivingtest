Ohio driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Ohio. It is written from Ohio Bureau of Motor Vehicles pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: At age 15 years and six months, an applicant may complete the knowledge test online at Ohio BMV - Online Services , or go to select deputy registrar locations or any driver exam station to take the knowledge test and vision screening, to begin the temporary permit process.
  • First license: Not clearly published on the pages we checked.
  • Written test: Applicants must pass both the knowledge test and vision screening to obtain a temporary instruction permit identification card (TIPIC).
  • Road test: Driver must pass the Driving and Skills tests.

Documents

  • Identity for a compliant card. Proof of full legal name, date of birth, legal presence, Social Security number, and two Ohio street-address documents is required for a compliant card. Source (official site)
  • Minor's co-signer. An unemancipated applicant under 18 must be accompanied by a responsible person who co-signs the permit. Source (official site)
  • Driver education certificate. A driver education certificate is provided at the driving and skills test. Source (official site)
  • Fifty-hour affidavit. The completed fifty-hour affidavit is brought to the driving test. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

An unexpired out-of-state non-commercial license can be exchanged at age 16 or older. If the out-of-state license is expired, or the person is a first-time driver, the testing process is required. The new-resident page does not say a current out-of-state license holder skips the knowledge or road test. Transfer rules (official site)

More detail is on the transfer guide.

Teen drivers

Driver must complete 50 hours of driving, with at least 10 hours of night driving, in addition to the driver education requirements. Practice hours (official site)

Hours, night rules, and passenger limits are on the teen licensing guide.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Ohio driver license or permit?

BMV publishes a document list that includes Identity for a compliant card, Minor's co-signer, Driver education certificate, Fifty-hour affidavit. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Ohio?

The BMV pages checked on 16 Nov 2026 do not clearly say that a learner permit from another state is accepted. Confirm with BMV before you drive or apply.

How do I book the Ohio road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through BMV, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are Ohio license and permit fees listed?

BMV publishes these amounts: Temporary instruction permit, operator $26.50. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
