Ohio teen driver license rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Teen licensing in Ohio is the graduated path published by Ohio Bureau of Motor Vehicles. Adult applicants can have different rules.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: At age 15 years and six months, an applicant may complete the knowledge test online at Ohio BMV - Online Services , or go to select deputy registrar locations or any driver exam station to take the knowledge test and vision screening, to begin the temporary permit process.
  • Full license: After the First 12 Months of Holding a License and Under Age 18.
  • Practice hours: Driver must complete 50 hours of driving, with at least 10 hours of night driving, in addition to the driver education requirements.
  • Night rule: You may not operate a vehicle from midnight - 6 a.m. unless:.
  • Passengers: A probationary driver may not carry more than one non-family passenger unless a parent or guardian is in the vehicle.
