Arizona driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Arizona. It is written from Arizona Revised Statutes pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: A person who is at least fifteen years and six months of age may apply to the department for an instruction permit for a class D or G license.
  • First license: The person is at least sixteen years of age.
  • Written test: The department may issue an instruction permit to the applicant after the applicant successfully passes all parts of the examination other than the driving test.
  • Road test: An actual demonstration of ability to exercise ordinary and reasonable control in the operation of a vehicle or vehicle combination of the type covered by the license classification or endorsement for which the applicant applies.

Documents

  • Name, date of birth, sex, and Arizona address. The application must include satisfactory proof of full legal name, date of birth, sex, and an Arizona domicile address if the applicant has one. Source (official site)
  • Social Security number. The application states the applicant's Social Security number. Source (official site)
  • Lawful presence. A license or identification license is not issued or renewed without proof that presence in the United States is authorized under federal law. Source (official site)

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Arizona driver license or permit?

Arizona Legislature publishes a document list that includes Name, date of birth, sex, and Arizona address, Social Security number, Lawful presence. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Arizona?

The Arizona Legislature pages checked on 16 Nov 2026 do not clearly say that a learner permit from another state is accepted. Confirm with Arizona Legislature before you drive or apply.

How do I book the Arizona road test?

A public scheduling link was not confirmed on the pages we checked. Start at the Arizona Legislature site linked here and follow their appointment steps.

Where are Arizona license and permit fees listed?

Arizona Legislature publishes these amounts: Instruction permit under section 28-3154 or 28-3156 seven dollars; Class D, G, or M license, age 39 or younger $25.00; Duplicate instruction permit two dollars. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
