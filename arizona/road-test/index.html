Arizona road test requirements

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

An actual demonstration of ability to exercise ordinary and reasonable control in the operation of a vehicle or vehicle combination of the type covered by the license classification or endorsement for which the applicant applies. Road test (official site)

Quick answer

The original-license examination includes a driving demonstration. The department may instead accept documentation of an approved driver training course or lessons from an instructor certified by the superintendent of public instruction.
