Virginia driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Virginia. It is written from Virginia Department of Motor Vehicles pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: You must be at least 15 years and 6 months of age.
  • First license: At least 16 years and 3 months of age.
  • Written test: you will need to take a vision screening and pass a two-part knowledge exam
  • Road test: you must complete the road skills test through your driver's education program.

Documents

  • Identity, legal presence, and residency. A learner-permit applicant submits one proof of identity, one proof of legal presence, and two proofs of Virginia residency. Source (official site)
  • Social Security number. Proof of the Social Security number is required if one has been issued, unless DMV can verify it electronically. Source (official site)
  • Parent consent. A parent or legal guardian signs the application for an applicant under 18. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

New residents have 60 days to get a Virginia license. Time already held on an out-of-state learner permit counts toward Virginia's holding period. Transfer rules (official site)

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Virginia driver license or permit?

DMV publishes a document list that includes Identity, legal presence, and residency, Social Security number, Parent consent. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Virginia?

Time on a valid out-of-state learner permit counts toward Virginia's minimum holding period.

How do I book the Virginia road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through DMV, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are Virginia license and permit fees listed?

DMV publishes these amounts: Standard eight-year driver license $32; REAL ID surcharge $10. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
