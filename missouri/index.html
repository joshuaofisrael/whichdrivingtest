Missouri driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Missouri. It is written from Missouri Department of Revenue pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: minimum age to apply for a Class F instruction permit? 15 years of age.
  • First license: minimum age to apply for an intermediate license? 16 years old.
  • Written test: You must pass the vision, road sign recognition , and written tests at a Missouri State Highway Patrol driver examination station .
  • Road test: You must pass the driving test at a Missouri State Highway Patrol driver examination station .

At a glance

Learner permitminimum age to apply for a Class F instruction permit? 15 years of age.
Written testYou must pass the vision, road sign recognition , and written tests at a Missouri State Highway Patrol driver examination station .
Supervised drivingYou must have received 40 hours of driving instruction, including a minimum of 10 hours of nighttime driving instruction between sunset and sunrise
Road testYou must pass the driving test at a Missouri State Highway Patrol driver examination station .
First licenseminimum age to apply for an intermediate license? 16 years old.
Full licenseAt age 18, or within the 30 days immediately preceding his/her 18th birthday, the intermediate license holder may apply for a full driver license.
REAL IDMissouri offers a REAL ID-compliant driver license or nondriver ID with a star, and a noncompliant card marked not for REAL ID purposes. The transaction and processing fees match the noncompliant credential. Document guide
FeesClass F instruction permit: $10; Class F intermediate license: $14; Class F driver license: $16.50; Class F driver license: $33 Fee details

Documents

  • Permission signature. A qualified person goes to the license office with the applicant and signs a permission statement. Source (official site)
  • Identity, lawful status, Social Security number, and residency. A new resident with an out-of-state license provides identity, lawful status, Social Security number, and a Missouri residential address. Source (official site)
  • Two residency documents for REAL ID. A REAL ID driver license or nondriver license needs two residency documents from two different sources. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

After establishing residency, a driver applies for a Missouri license. Surrendering a valid out-of-state license, or one expired 184 days or less, can waive the Missouri skills and written tests. Vision and road-sign tests are still required. Transfer rules (official site)

More detail is on the transfer guide.

Teen drivers

First-time drivers ages 15 through 18 complete an instruction-permit period and an intermediate-license period before a full driver license. The intermediate license allows driving alone except for a late-night curfew and passenger limits. Teen rules (official site)

Hours, night rules, and passenger limits are on the teen licensing guide.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Missouri driver license or permit?

DOR publishes a document list that includes Permission signature, Identity, lawful status, Social Security number, and residency, Two residency documents for REAL ID. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Missouri?

The DOR pages checked on 16 Nov 2026 do not clearly say that a learner permit from another state is accepted. Confirm with DOR before you drive or apply.

How do I book the Missouri road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through DOR, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are Missouri license and permit fees listed?

DOR publishes these amounts: Class F instruction permit $10; Class F intermediate license $14; Class F driver license $16.50; Class F driver license $33. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
