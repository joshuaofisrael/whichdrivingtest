Missouri teen driver license rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Teen licensing in Missouri is the graduated path published by Missouri Department of Revenue. Adult applicants can have different rules.

First-time drivers ages 15 through 18 complete an instruction-permit period and an intermediate-license period before a full driver license. The intermediate license allows driving alone except for a late-night curfew and passenger limits. Teen rules (official site)

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: minimum age to apply for a Class F instruction permit? 15 years of age.
  • First license: minimum age to apply for an intermediate license? 16 years old.
  • Full license: At age 18, or within the 30 days immediately preceding his/her 18th birthday, the intermediate license holder may apply for a full driver license.
  • Practice hours: You must have received 40 hours of driving instruction, including a minimum of 10 hours of nighttime driving instruction between sunset and sunrise.
  • Night rule: An intermediate driver may not drive alone from 1 a.m. to 5 a.m. except to or from a school activity or job, for an emergency, or with a licensed driver who is at least 21.
  • Passengers: For the first 6 months of an intermediate license, the driver may not carry more than one passenger under 19 who is not immediate family. After that, the limit is three passengers under 19 who are not immediate family.
