North Carolina driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in North Carolina. It is written from North Carolina Division of Motor Vehicles pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: If you are at least 15 years of age and have completed an approved driver education course that meets North Carolina requirements, you may apply for a Level One Limited Learner Permit to operate vehicles requiring a Class C License.
  • First license: Drivers must be at least 16 years old, but less than 18.
  • Written test: You must pass written, sign and vision tests.
  • Road test: Once you have completed the above, you can take a road test to get your Level 2 Limited Provisional License.

Documents

  • Identity and date of birth. Bring one document that verifies identity and date of birth. Source (official site)
  • Social Security. Bring a Social Security card or another document proving a Social Security number. Source (official site)
  • North Carolina residency. Bring one document proving North Carolina residency. Source (official site)
  • Driver education certificate. A driver education certificate is required before the limited learner permit. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

A new resident has 60 days after establishing residence to get a North Carolina license or learner permit. A new resident applying for a Class C license may have to take knowledge, vision, signs, and, if the examiner requires it, a driving skills test. Transfer rules (official site)

More detail is on the transfer guide.

Teen drivers

To be eligible, you must be at least 15 years old and have received a Driver's Education Certificate from an approved driver education course. Teen rules (official site)

Hours, night rules, and passenger limits are on the teen licensing guide.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a North Carolina driver license or permit?

NCDMV publishes a document list that includes Identity and date of birth, Social Security, North Carolina residency, Driver education certificate. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in North Carolina?

A new resident who is at least 15 and under 18 and already has a learner permit, restricted license, or unrestricted license is told to contact a local office to learn which North Carolina credential they may apply for. The handbook does not state that the other state's permit is accepted as a North Carolina permit.

How do I book the North Carolina road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through NCDMV, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are North Carolina license and permit fees listed?

NCDMV publishes these amounts: Level 1 limited learner permit $25.50; Class C license $6.50/year. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
