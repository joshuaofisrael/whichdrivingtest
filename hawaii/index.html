This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Hawaii. It is written from Hawaii Department of Transportation pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: The minimum age to acquire an instruction permit is 15 1/2 years.
  • First license: The minimum age to obtain a provisional license is 16 years.
  • Written test: taking a vision screening test and a rules of the road test
  • Road test: You must pass a road test

Documents

  • Legal name, date of birth, and legal presence. Common examples in the manual include a birth certificate or valid U.S. passport. Source (official site)
  • Social Security number. The manual lists a Social Security card as the example for the Social Security number. Source (official site)
  • Two proofs of Hawaii residence. Two documents must show the applicant's name and principal residence address in Hawaii. Source (official site)
  • Driver education certificates under 18. A state-certified course and both classroom and behind-the-wheel certificates are required for the provisional license. Source (official site)
  • Parental consent under 18. Applicants younger than 18 need written parental consent unless married or emancipated. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Hawaii driver license or permit?

HDOT publishes a document list that includes Legal name, date of birth, and legal presence, Social Security number, Two proofs of Hawaii residence, Driver education certificates under 18, Parental consent under 18. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Hawaii?

The HDOT pages checked on 16 Nov 2026 do not clearly say that a learner permit from another state is accepted. Confirm with HDOT before you drive or apply.

How do I book the Hawaii road test?

A public scheduling link was not confirmed on the pages we checked. Start at the HDOT site linked here and follow their appointment steps.

Where are Hawaii license and permit fees listed?

A dollar amount was not confirmed for this guide, so none is shown. The official fee page is linked here. Pay HDOT directly.
