This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Arkansas. It is written from Arkansas Department of Finance and Administration, Office of Driver Services pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: Learner's license is for ages 14-15.
  • First license: Intermediate license is for ages 16-17.
  • Written test: Not clearly published on the pages we checked.
  • Road test: Not clearly published on the pages we checked.

Documents

  • New license identity set. A new driver license or state ID requires proof of legal presence, proof of identity, and a Social Security card, plus a vision exam and photo. Source (official site)
  • REAL ID residency. REAL ID residency requires two qualifying documents, such as a utility bill, recent paycheck, bank statement, tax return, or insurance policy. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Arkansas driver license or permit?

DFA publishes a document list that includes New license identity set, REAL ID residency. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Arkansas?

The DFA pages checked on 16 Nov 2026 do not clearly say that a learner permit from another state is accepted. Confirm with DFA before you drive or apply.

How do I book the Arkansas road test?

A public scheduling link was not confirmed on the pages we checked. Start at the DFA site linked here and follow their appointment steps.

Where are Arkansas license and permit fees listed?

DFA publishes these amounts: REAL ID with a new eight-year term $40; REAL ID keeping the current expiration ten dollars. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
