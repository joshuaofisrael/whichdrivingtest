Indiana learner permit requirements

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

In Indiana, Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles publishes You may obtain a learner's permit if you are at least 15 years old and enrolled in an approved behind-the-wheel training course. as the minimum age for a learner permit. Permit age (official site)

Quick answer

  • Minimum age: You may obtain a learner's permit if you are at least 15 years old and enrolled in an approved behind-the-wheel training course.
  • Holding period: 180 days.
  • Practice hours: complete at least 50 hours of supervised driving practice with a licensed instructor or.
