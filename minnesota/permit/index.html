Minnesota learner permit requirements

In Minnesota, Minnesota Department of Public Safety publishes The department may issue an instruction permit to an applicant who is 15, 16, or 17 years of age and who: as the minimum age for a learner permit. Permit age (official site)

Quick answer

  • Minimum age: The department may issue an instruction permit to an applicant who is 15, 16, or 17 years of age and who:.
  • Holding period: not less than six months.
  • Practice hours: at least 50 hours behind the wheel supervised by a parent or guardian, including 15 hours at night.
