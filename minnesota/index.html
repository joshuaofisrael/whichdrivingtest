This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Minnesota. It is written from Minnesota Department of Public Safety pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: The department may issue an instruction permit to an applicant who is 15, 16, or 17 years of age and who:.
  • First license: has reached the age of 16 years;.
  • Written test: has passed a department-administered test of the applicant's knowledge of traffic laws;
  • Road test: To take the road test, teen drivers under 18 must spend at least 50 hours behind the wheel supervised by a parent or guardian, including 15 hours at night.

Documents

  • Parent, guardian, or other authorized approval. A minor's instruction-permit application must be approved by a parent, guardian, foster parent, spouse, or another person the statute lists. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

Time already spent holding an instruction permit from another jurisdiction is credited toward Minnesota's permit holding period. Transfer rules (official site)

More detail is on the transfer guide.

Teen drivers

An instruction permit is available at 15, 16, or 17 after driver education steps, vision and knowledge tests, and an approved application. A provisional license is available at 16 after the permit has been held and a supervised driving log is submitted. The first six months of that provisional license limit late-night driving and young passengers. Teen rules (official site)

Hours, night rules, and passenger limits are on the teen licensing guide.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Minnesota driver license or permit?

DPS publishes a document list that includes Parent, guardian, or other authorized approval. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Minnesota?

Time on an instruction permit from another jurisdiction counts toward the Minnesota holding period.

How do I book the Minnesota road test?

A public scheduling link was not confirmed on the pages we checked. Start at the DPS site linked here and follow their appointment steps.

Where are Minnesota license and permit fees listed?

DPS publishes these amounts: Driver and vehicle services technology surcharge $2.25. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
