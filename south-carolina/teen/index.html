South Carolina teen driver license rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Teen licensing in South Carolina is the graduated path published by South Carolina Department of Motor Vehicles. Adult applicants can have different rules.

The graduated driver's license program is designed for people 15 to 17. Teen rules (official site)

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: 15 to apply for a regular or motorcycle.
  • First license: If you are at least 15 1/2, but less than 16,.
  • Full license: full driving privileges at 17, or after keeping your Conditional or Special Restricted license for one year, as long as you received no traffic offenses and were not at-fault in any collisions.
  • Practice hours: You have practiced driving with a qualified, licensed individual for at least 40 hours, including ten hours of night driving.
  • Night rule: You may drive alone from 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. (8 p.m. during daylight savings time).
  • Passengers: A conditional or special restricted driver cannot have more than two passengers under 21 unless a licensed adult who is at least 21 is in the car, with an exception for family members or students traveling to and from school.
