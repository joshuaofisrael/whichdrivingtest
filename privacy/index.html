Privacy

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Last updated 16 Nov 2026.

This policy explains how Which Driving Test (whichdrivingtest.com), a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company, handles information.

Analytics

This build does not load an analytics beacon. When the public domain is live, the site is set up to use Cloudflare Web Analytics. That tool does not use cookies or local storage and does not track you across sites. It records aggregate details such as the page, referrer, browser, and country.

Email

If you email the address on the contact page, we use your message and your address to reply. We do not sell that information. We keep it only as long as needed to handle the message.

Advertising

The site shows no ads today. If we add ads later, for example through Google AdSense, third party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. You would be able to opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info/choices. Where the law requires it, we will ask for consent before advertising cookies are set. We will update this policy before any ads go live.

Cookies

Which Driving Test itself sets no cookies. The site works if your browser blocks cookies.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security and operations.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing the contact address. Residents of some states and countries may have additional rights, which we will honor.

Children

The site is a general audience reference. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new date.
