Privacy

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Last updated 19 Nov 2026.

This policy explains how Which Driving Test (whichdrivingtest.com), a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company, handles information.

Analytics

Published pages load two measurement tools. Neither one is a state DMV.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is Cloudflare's cookieless beacon. The page loads https://static.cloudflareinsights.com/beacon.min.js with a public site token. It does not use cookies and does not track you across sites. It records aggregate details such as the page, referrer, browser, and country.

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is Google's measurement product. It stays off until you choose Accept on the consent banner. Before that choice, the page sets Google Consent Mode v2 defaults to denied for ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization, and analytics storage. After you accept, the site loads the GA4 loader at https://www.googletagmanager.com/gtag/js and Google may set cookies. Reject keeps analytics storage denied. Cookie settings in the footer reopens the choice. The measurement id is a G- id the site owner adds in the build config. Until that id is filled in, accepting does not send a hit.

Email

If you email the address on the contact page, we use your message and your address to reply. We do not sell that information. We keep it only as long as needed to handle the message.

Advertising

Which Driving Test uses a Google AdSense account meta tag and the AdSense loader script so the publisher account can be verified. The site does not place ad units on pages today, so you should not see display ads while you read. If ads are approved and shown later, third party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info/choices. Where the law requires it, we will ask for consent before advertising cookies are set. See the advertising disclosure.

Google advertising cookies and your choices

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Cookies

Which Driving Test stores your analytics choice in local storage in this browser so the banner does not ask on every page. That choice is not an account. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not set a cookie. Google Analytics 4 may set cookies only after you accept. The site still works if you reject, or if your browser blocks cookies.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security and operations.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing the contact address. Residents of some states and countries may have additional rights, which we will honor.

Children

The site is a general audience reference. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new date.
