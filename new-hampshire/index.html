New Hampshire driving test requirements and DMV guide

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in New Hampshire. It is written from New Hampshire Department of Safety pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: Not clearly published on the pages we checked.
  • First license: a youth operator's license to applicants who are 16 years of age or older and under 21 years of age.
  • Written test: shall include knowledge questions regarding distracted driving, driving under the influence, and driving during poor weather conditions
  • Road test: Not clearly published on the pages we checked.

Documents

  • Driver education certificate under 18. present a certificate of successful completion of a driver education course Source (official site)
  • 40-hour supervised driving certification under 18. at least 10 of the hours of additional supervised driving time were during the period from 1/2 hour after sunset Source (official site)

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a New Hampshire driver license or permit?

DOS publishes a document list that includes Driver education certificate under 18, 40-hour supervised driving certification under 18. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in New Hampshire?

The DOS pages checked on 16 Nov 2026 do not clearly say that a learner permit from another state is accepted. Confirm with DOS before you drive or apply.

How do I book the New Hampshire road test?

A public scheduling link was not confirmed on the pages we checked. Start at the DOS site linked here and follow their appointment steps.

Where are New Hampshire license and permit fees listed?

DOS publishes these amounts: Original or renewal Real ID Act compliant license $60; Other original or renewal non-commercial license $50; Youth operator license and examination $10 per year, not to exceed $50. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
