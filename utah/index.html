Utah driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Utah. It is written from Utah Driver License Division pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: To apply for a learner permit, you must be at least 15 years of age.
  • First license: To apply for a regular Class D license, you must be at least 16 years of age.
  • Written test: Required to pass a 50-question closed-book test.
  • Road test: In Utah, before you can be licensed for the first time, you must pass a driving skills test.

Documents

  • Birth certificate. One identity option is a certified birth certificate from the state vital-records office. Source (official site)
  • Social Security card. A signed Social Security card is one accepted proof of the Social Security number. Source (official site)
  • Utah address. Two documents showing name and address, dated within 90 days, prove a Utah address. Source (official site)

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Utah driver license or permit?

DLD publishes a document list that includes Birth certificate, Social Security card, Utah address. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Utah?

An out-of-state learner permit can count toward Utah's six-month holding period.

How do I book the Utah road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through DLD, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are Utah license and permit fees listed?

DLD publishes these amounts: Original learner permit, age 20 and under $19.00; Original provisional Class D license, 5-year $39.00. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
