Utah teen driver license rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Teen licensing in Utah is the graduated path published by Utah Driver License Division. Adult applicants can have different rules.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: To apply for a learner permit, you must be at least 15 years of age.
  • First license: To apply for a regular Class D license, you must be at least 16 years of age.
  • Full license: Licensed Drivers 16-17 Years of Age Nighttime Driving Restrictions No driving between the hours of midnight and 5 am.
  • Practice hours: 40 hours, including 10 after sunset.
  • Night rule: No driving between the hours of midnight and 5 am unless accompanied by a licensed driver.
  • Passengers: For the first six months or until age 18, you cannot drive with non-immediate family members.
