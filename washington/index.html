Washington driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Washington. It is written from Washington State Department of Licensing pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: You can apply for a permit as soon as you turn 15.
  • First license: To get your first driver license, you must: Be at least 16 years old.
  • Written test: You must get 32 out of 40 correct answers to pass the test.
  • Road test: Pass the knowledge and drive tests

Documents

  • Identity. A valid U.S. passport or passport card is a stand-alone identity document. Source (official site)
  • Social Security number. Applicants provide a Social Security number if they have one. Source (official site)
  • Parent or guardian at the office. You must either bring a parent with you to the licensing office or have them fill out the Parental Authorization Affidavit Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

A driver 18 or older with a valid license from another U.S. state does not take knowledge or drive tests. Drivers under 18 follow the teen transfer steps. Transfer rules (official site)

More detail is on the transfer guide.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Washington driver license or permit?

DOL publishes a document list that includes Identity, Social Security number, Parent or guardian at the office. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Washington?

The six-month permit period can include time on an out-of-state learner permit.

How do I book the Washington road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through DOL, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are Washington license and permit fees listed?

DOL publishes these amounts: Instruction permit $35 for 1 year; Renew a learner permit $25 for 1 year. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
