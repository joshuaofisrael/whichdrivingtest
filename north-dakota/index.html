North Dakota driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in North Dakota. It is written from North Dakota Department of Transportation pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: If starting at age 14: Must hold the permit for at least 12 months.
  • First license: restricted license at the age of 15.
  • Written test: Pass vision and written knowledge tests at a NDDOT licensing facility.
  • Road test: Must successfully complete a road test through the state to become fully licensed.

Documents

  • Identity and address. A learner's permit application requires proof of identity and address. Source (official site)

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a North Dakota driver license or permit?

NDDOT publishes a document list that includes Identity and address, Sponsor signature under 18. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in North Dakota?

The NDDOT pages checked on 16 Nov 2026 do not clearly say that a learner permit from another state is accepted. Confirm with NDDOT before you drive or apply.

How do I book the North Dakota road test?

A public scheduling link was not confirmed on the pages we checked. Start at the NDDOT site linked here and follow their appointment steps.

Where are North Dakota license and permit fees listed?

NDDOT publishes these amounts: Knowledge test $5. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
