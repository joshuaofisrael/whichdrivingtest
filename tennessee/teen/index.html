Tennessee teen driver license rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Teen licensing in Tennessee is the graduated path published by Tennessee Department of Safety and Homeland Security. Adult applicants can have different rules.

The graduated program starts with a learner permit, then an intermediate restricted license after the permit is held and a road skills test is passed, then an intermediate unrestricted license, and later a regular Class D license. Teen rules (official site)

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: You must be 15 years old and pass the standard written exams and vision screening.
  • First license: To earn the GDL Level 2 - Intermediate Restricted License, drivers must be 16 years old and pass the road skills test.
  • Full license: To earn the GDL Level 3 - Intermediate Unrestricted License, drivers must be 17 years old and must have held a GDL Level 2 - Intermediate Restricted License for one year.
  • Practice hours: Must have 50 hours of behind the wheel driving experience including 10 hours of night driving.
  • Night rule: An intermediate restricted license holder may not drive between 11 p.m. and 6 a.m.
  • Passengers: An intermediate restricted license holder may have only one passenger.

The learner permit starts at 15 after the written exams and vision screening. Teen rules (official site)

A learner-permit holder may not drive between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m. Teen rules (official site)

The intermediate restricted step requires 50 practice hours, including 10 at night. Teen rules (official site)

With an intermediate restricted license, only one passenger is allowed, and driving is not allowed between 11 p.m. and 6 a.m. Teen rules (official site)

The intermediate unrestricted license is at 17 after one year on the intermediate restricted license. Teen rules (official site)

A regular Class D license is available at 18, or at high school graduation or a GED, whichever is sooner. Teen rules (official site)
