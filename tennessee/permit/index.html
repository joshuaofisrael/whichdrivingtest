Tennessee learner permit requirements

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

In Tennessee, Tennessee Department of Safety and Homeland Security publishes You must be 15 years old and pass the standard written exams and vision screening. as the minimum age for a learner permit. Permit age (official site)

Quick answer

  • Minimum age: You must be 15 years old and pass the standard written exams and vision screening.
  • Holding period: 180 days.
  • Practice hours: Must have 50 hours of behind the wheel driving experience including 10 hours of night driving.
