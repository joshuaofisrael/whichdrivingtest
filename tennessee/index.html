Tennessee driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Tennessee. It is written from Tennessee Department of Safety and Homeland Security pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: You must be 15 years old and pass the standard written exams and vision screening.
  • First license: To earn the GDL Level 2 - Intermediate Restricted License, drivers must be 16 years old and pass the road skills test.
  • Written test: You must be 15 years old and pass the standard written exams and vision screening.
  • Road test: drivers must be 16 years old and pass the road skills test.

Documents

  • U.S. citizenship or legal presence. The applicant presents one proof of U.S. citizenship or legal presence. Source (official site)
  • Social Security number. A non-REAL ID applicant may state the Social Security number, which is checked with the Social Security Administration. Source (official site)
  • Parent or guardian signature. A parent or legal guardian must be present to sign the minor affidavit and show financial responsibility. Source (official site)
  • School attendance. The applicant shows school attendance from a Tennessee school or a signed letter from the previous school. Source (official site)
  • 50-hour driving certification. A parent, guardian, or driving instructor signs the certification of behind-the-wheel practice. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

A new resident who holds another state's driver license must get a Tennessee license within 30 days and surrender the out-of-state license. A license expired more than six months requires the vision screening, knowledge test, and road skills test. Transfer rules (official site)

More detail is on the transfer guide.

Teen drivers

The graduated program starts with a learner permit, then an intermediate restricted license after the permit is held and a road skills test is passed, then an intermediate unrestricted license, and later a regular Class D license. Teen rules (official site)

Hours, night rules, and passenger limits are on the teen licensing guide.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Tennessee driver license or permit?

DOS publishes a document list that includes U.S. citizenship or legal presence, Social Security number, Parent or guardian signature, School attendance, 50-hour driving certification. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Tennessee?

A new resident under 18 who holds a learner's permit from another state must meet separate teen graduated-license requirements.

How do I book the Tennessee road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through DOS, not a third party site that charges to file for you.
