Mississippi license transfer rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Any new Mississippi resident with an expired out of state license is required to take the knowledge exam. Transfer rules (official site)

Learner permit from another state

A valid out-of-state learner's permit is credited toward Mississippi's 12-month holding period. Permit from another state (official site)

Frequently asked questions

How do I transfer a license to Mississippi?

Any new Mississippi resident with an expired out of state license is required to take the knowledge exam.

Does Mississippi waive the tests for a new resident?

Only if the summary on this page says so. If it does not, the pages we checked did not make a waiver clear.

Can I drive in Mississippi on a learner permit from another state?

A valid out-of-state learner's permit is credited toward Mississippi's 12-month holding period.
