Nebraska teen driver license rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Teen licensing in Nebraska is the graduated path published by Nebraska Department of Motor Vehicles. Adult applicants can have different rules.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: the Learner's Permit cannot be issued until the day of your 15th birthday or after your 15th birthday.
  • First license: the permit will not be issued until on or after your 16th birthday.
  • Full license: renewing your Class O License. Minimum Age 17 Testing.
  • Practice hours: or Complete a 50 hour certification form signed by a parent, guardian or licensed driver who is at least 21.
  • Night rule: A provisional permit allows unsupervised driving from 6 a.m. to midnight. Unsupervised driving from midnight to 6 a.m. is limited to travel between home and work or a school activity.
  • Passengers: no more than one passenger who is not an immediate family member and who is under nineteen years of age.
