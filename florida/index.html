Florida driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Florida. It is written from Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: Must be at least 15 years old;.
  • First license: Must be at least 16 years old;.
  • Written test: To pass, a customer must answer 40 out of 50 questions correctly, or score 80 percent.
  • Road test: Must pass Class E Driving Skills Test;

Documents

  • Primary identification. U.S. citizens can use a valid U.S. passport or passport card as one primary identification document. Source (official site)
  • Social Security number. A Social Security card in the customer's current name is one accepted proof of the Social Security number. Source (official site)
  • Residential address. Two different documents must show a Florida residential address. Source (official site)

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Florida driver license or permit?

FLHSMV publishes a document list that includes Primary identification, Social Security number, Residential address. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Florida?

The FLHSMV pages checked on 16 Nov 2026 do not clearly say that a learner permit from another state is accepted. Confirm with FLHSMV before you drive or apply.

How do I book the Florida road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through FLHSMV, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are Florida license and permit fees listed?

FLHSMV publishes these amounts: Original Class E, including a learner license $48.00; Knowledge retest $10.00; Skill retest $20.00. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
