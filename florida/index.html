Florida driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Florida. It is written from Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: Must be at least 15 years old;.
  • First license: Must be at least 16 years old;.
  • Written test: The exam consists of 50 multiple-choice questions about traffic laws and traffic signs. The passing score is 80 percent or 40 out of 50 questions;
  • Road test: Must pass Class E Driving Skills Test;

Documents

  • Parental consent under 18. A parent or legal guardian must sign a notarized consent form. A step-parent may sign only after a legal adoption. Source (official site)
  • Driver education course. Proof of a Driver Education Traffic Safety course is required for the learner's license. Source (official site)
  • Identity, Social Security number, and address. The applicant must prove identity, Social Security number, and residential address. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

A new resident must get a Florida driver license within 30 days of establishing residency. The new-resident page does not say whether the knowledge or driving test is waived. Transfer rules (official site)

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Florida driver license or permit?

FLHSMV publishes a document list that includes Parental consent under 18, Driver education course, Identity, Social Security number, and address. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Florida?

The FLHSMV pages checked on 16 Nov 2026 do not clearly say that a learner permit from another state is accepted. Confirm with FLHSMV before you drive or apply.

How do I book the Florida road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through FLHSMV, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are Florida license and permit fees listed?

FLHSMV publishes these amounts: Original Class E, including a learner's license $48.00; Knowledge retest $10.00; Skill retest $20.00; Tax collector service fee that may apply to a credential $6.25. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
