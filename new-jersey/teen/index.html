New Jersey teen driver license rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Teen licensing in New Jersey is the graduated path published by New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission. Adult applicants can have different rules.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: you can obtain a special learner's permit at age 16.
  • First license: You cannot get your license until you are 17 years of age.
  • Full license: when you turn 18 years old, you should upgrade your probationary license to a basic license.
  • Practice hours: Supervised driving must include a minimum of 50 practice hours including 10 hours during darkness.
  • Night rule: May not operate a motor vehicle after 11:01 p.m. and before 5:00 a.m.
  • Passengers: One additional passenger unless a parent or guardian is in the vehicle.
