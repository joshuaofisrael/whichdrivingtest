New Jersey learner permit requirements

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

In New Jersey, New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission publishes you can obtain a special learner's permit at age 16. as the minimum age for a learner permit. Permit age (official site)

Quick answer

  • Minimum age: you can obtain a special learner's permit at age 16.
  • Holding period: 6 months.
  • Practice hours: Supervised driving must include a minimum of 50 practice hours including 10 hours during darkness.
