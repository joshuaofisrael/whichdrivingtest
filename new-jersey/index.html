This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in New Jersey. It is written from New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: If you are enrolled in a behind the wheel driver training course at either of the above you can obtain a special learner's permit at age 16.
  • First license: You cannot get your license until you are 17 years of age.
  • Written test: You will take your knowledge test at the school. You will also be required to take a vision test.
  • Road test: Make an appointment for your road test for 6 months after you pass your knowledge test.

Documents

  • Six points of identification. Standard license applicants present documents worth 6 points that prove age and identity. Source (official site)
  • Address and Social Security number. The special-learner permit purchase also uses proof of residential address and a Social Security number. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

New residents must transfer an out-of-state license within 60 days or before it expires, whichever comes first. Knowledge and road tests are waived for a current, valid, non-probationary license from a U.S. state, D.C., or listed U.S. territory. The license is surrendered when New Jersey issues its license. Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a New Jersey driver license or permit?

MVC publishes a document list that includes Six points of identification, Address and Social Security number. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in New Jersey?

Drivers under 18 who move to New Jersey are subject to the GDL program and follow the special or examination permit steps. The manual does not say another state's learner permit replaces a New Jersey permit.

How do I book the New Jersey road test?

A public scheduling link was not confirmed on the pages we checked. Start at the MVC site linked here and follow their appointment steps.

Where are New Jersey license and permit fees listed?

MVC publishes these amounts: Probationary-to-basic upgrade, usual prorated fee $19.50; Out-of-state transfer permit $10. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
