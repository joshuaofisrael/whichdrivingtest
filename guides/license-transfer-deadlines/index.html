How many days after a move must you switch your license?

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 21 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

People want one number of days. The states do not share one. Pages opened on 21 Nov 2026 showed 9 states on a 30 day clock, 7 on 60 days, and 3 on 90 days. Tennessee is an extra 30 day row, kept from 16 Nov 2026, because that agency site did not open today. Wyoming prints one year, with a short list of exceptions. A neighbor is often a different number. Maine is 30 days. Connecticut is 90. Ohio is 30. Indiana is 60. Virginia is 60. West Virginia is 30.

A deadline cell is included only when the official page states it. A cell that says the deadline was not clearly published is not a guess that you may wait. Tennessee's 30 days is kept from this site's transfer guide, last checked 16 Nov 2026, because the agency site did not open today. The permit holding period is a different clock. That one is how long a learner permit must be held before a road test. Teen night rules and passenger limits are on the teen licensing table. The REAL ID guide is the federal card rule, not a move deadline.

Frequently asked questions

Is the number of days the same in every state?

No. Pages opened on 21 Nov 2026 showed 30, 60, and 90 day clocks, plus one year in Wyoming. A blank cell means that page did not state a number.

Does a commercial license use the same deadline?

Often no. Alaska, Maryland, Virginia, and Wisconsin print a shorter commercial clock on the same page as the ordinary license. The note on that row is the place to read it.

What does Wyoming's one year rule actually say?

You have one year after you establish residency, unless the license you already hold is from Georgia, Massachusetts, Michigan, Tennessee, or Wisconsin, or it is a commercial license. Those exceptions apply as soon as residency is established.

Why is a state missing from the table?

Massachusetts, Illinois, Colorado, and New York are not in the table because the official sites could not be quoted. A published state with a blank deadline had no day count on the page that was opened.
