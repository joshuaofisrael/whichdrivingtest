Teen driver licensing by state

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Massachusetts, Illinois, Colorado, and New York are not yet covered because the official sites could not be quoted.

Graduated licensing rules for teens differ by state. This table shows practice hours, night rules, and passenger limits only when the state agency publishes them.

StatePractice hoursNight rulePassengers
Alabamaverifying that the applicant has COMPLETED a minimum of 50 hours of behind-the-wheel practice.A Stage II driver must not drive from midnight to 6 a.m. unless accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, or by a licensed driver age 21 or older with consent, or while traveling for specified school, work, religious, or emergency reasons.A Stage II driver must not have more than one passenger other than parents, legal guardians, or family members.
AlaskaNot clearly published on the pages we checked.You may not drive between 1:00 & 5:00 a.m.A provisional license holder may not carry passengers under 21, except siblings.
Arizonathe applicant has completed at least thirty hours of supervised driving practice and that at least ten of the required practice hours were at night.For the first six months, a class G licensee shall not drive from 12:00 a.m. to 5:00 a.m. unless a licensed parent or guardian is in the front seat or the trip is directly to or from school, work, a religious activity, or a family emergency.the licensee shall not drive a motor vehicle on a public highway at any time if the licensee is driving a motor vehicle containing more than one passenger under the age of eighteen.
ArkansasNot clearly published on the pages we checked.Not clearly published on the pages we checked.Not clearly published on the pages we checked.
CaliforniaPractice driving for at least 50 hours with a California-licensed driver who is at least 25 years old. Ten hours must be at night.During the first 12 months of a provisional license, driving is not allowed between 11 p.m. and 5 a.m., with exceptions that require a signed note.With passengers under 20 years old, unless your parent or guardian or other California-licensed driver (at least 25 years old) rides with you.
Connecticut40 hoursUntil the driver's eighteenth birthday , they may not drive between hours of 11 p.m. - 5 a.m.First six months: only a parent, legal guardian, driving instructor, or qualified trainer
Delawarethe permit holder has completed 50 hours of driving time, which shall include 10 hours of nighttime driving.After the first six months, the permit holder may drive unsupervised between 6:00 a.m. and 10:00 p.m.When supervision is required, no person other than the supervising driver may sit in the front seat.
District of ColumbiaNot clearly published on the pages we checked.A DC DMV learner permit entitles you to drive between the hours of 6:00 am and 9:00 pm.The GRAD page says there are no passenger restrictions for full-license drivers once they turn 18.
Floridacertifying the driver has 50 hours of driving experience, of which 10 hours must be at night.Driving is only allowed between 6 a.m. and 11 p.m.Not clearly published on the pages we checked.
Georgia40 hours, including 6 at nightClass D: no driving from midnight to 5 a.m.During the second six months of a Class D license, only one passenger under 21 who is not immediate family may ride.
HawaiiNot clearly published on the pages we checked.With a provisional license, driving from 11:00 p.m. to 5:00 a.m. requires a licensed parent or guardian, unless the trip is to or from work or a school-authorized activity.A provisional license allows only one passenger under 18 who is not a household member, unless a parent or guardian is along.
IdahoFifty (50) hours of supervised driving (10 of the 50 hours must be at night)Drivers under the age of 16 are restricted to driving during daylight hours onlyFor the first six months after a license is issued, a driver under 17 may have no more than one passenger under 17, unless those passengers are related by blood, adoption, or marriage.
Indianacomplete at least 50 hours of supervised driving practice with a licensed instructor orFor 180 days after the probationary license, driving is not allowed between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m., with exceptions for work, school, religious events, or a qualifying adult.For 180 days after the probationary license, the driver may not carry passengers unless a licensed instructor, a licensed driver at least 25, or a spouse at least 21 is in the front seat. The driver's child, stepchild, sibling, step or half sibling, or spouse may ride without that extra adult.
IowaComplete 10 hours of supervised driving while holding your intermediate license.Drive without adult supervision between 5 a.m.If accepted, no more than one unrelated minor passenger during the first six months of unsupervised intermediate driving
Kansasat 15 must have at least 25 hours; must provide 50 prior to 16 to move to lesser restrictionsNot clearly published on the pages we checked.Passenger restriction: No
KentuckyComplete at least 60 hours of supervised driving practice, including 10 hours completed at night.Not clearly published on the pages we checked.While practicing on the permit, a licensed driver age 21 or older must be in the front passenger seat.
LouisianaNot clearly published on the pages we checked.The student information page says an intermediate driver may drive from 11 p.m. until 5 a.m. with a licensed family member 18 or older or anyone over 21, and that the license otherwise reverts to a learner's permit.The student information page says an intermediate driver may drive with one sibling passenger from 6 p.m. until 11 p.m.
Mainecomplete 70 hours of practice driving (PDF) , which must include 10 hours of night drivingOperating a motor vehicle between the hours of midnight (12 a.m.) and 5 a.m.An intermediate license holder may not carry passengers other than immediate family unless a qualified licensed operator is in the vehicle.
MarylandComplete 60 supervised practice driving hours , including at least 10 nighttime hoursA provisional driver may not drive between midnight and 5 a.m. unless an exception on the provisional page applies.For the first 151 days, a provisional driver under 18 may not drive with passengers under 18 unless they are immediate family, unless a qualified supervising driver is in the car.
MichiganTeens are required to keep a driving log and complete a minimum of 50 hours of supervised driving (10 hours at nighttime) before they can take the driving skills test, which is required for the Level 2 License.Shall not operate a motor vehicle between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m. except when:Shall not operate a motor vehicle at any time with more than 1 passenger in the vehicle who is younger than 21 years of age except:
Minnesotaat least 50 hours behind the wheel supervised by a parent or guardian, including 15 hours at night.For the first six months, a provisional license holder may drive between midnight and 5:00 a.m. only in the situations the statute lists, including trips between home and work.For the first six months, a provisional license holder may not carry more than one passenger under 20 who is not immediate family. The limit does not apply when a parent or guardian is in the vehicle.
MississippiNot clearly published on the pages we checked.Not clearly published on the pages we checked.Not clearly published on the pages we checked.
MissouriYou must have received 40 hours of driving instruction, including a minimum of 10 hours of nighttime driving instruction between sunset and sunriseAn intermediate driver may not drive alone from 1 a.m. to 5 a.m. except to or from a school activity or job, for an emergency, or with a licensed driver who is at least 21.For the first 6 months of an intermediate license, the driver may not carry more than one passenger under 19 who is not immediate family. After that, the limit is three passengers under 19 who are not immediate family.
Montanathe person has had at least 50 hours of driving experience, 10 of which were at nightmay not operate a motor vehicle between the hours of 11 p.m. and 5 a.m. unless the restricted licensee is:For the first six months of the one-year restriction, a restricted licensee may not drive with more than one passenger under 18 unless supervised by a licensed driver who is at least 18 or the extra passengers are family members.
Nebraskaor Complete a 50 hour certification form signed by a parent, guardian or licensed driver who is at least 21A provisional permit allows unsupervised driving from 6 a.m. to midnight. Unsupervised driving from midnight to 6 a.m. is limited to travel between home and work or a school activity.no more than one passenger who is not an immediate family member and who is under nineteen years of age
Nevada50 hours, including 10 at nightNo driving from 10 p.m. to 5 a.m., except travel to or from school or workFirst 6 months: no passengers under 18 except immediate family
New Hampshirecompletion of 40 hours of additional supervised driving timeshall not operate a motor vehicle between the hours of 1:00 a.m. and 4:00 a.m.For the first six months, a youth operator may not carry more than one passenger under 25 who is not a family member, unless a licensed adult who is at least 25 is in the vehicle.
New JerseySupervised driving must include a minimum of 50 practice hours including 10 hours during darkness.May not operate a motor vehicle after 11:01 p.m. and before 5:00 a.m.One additional passenger unless accompanied by a parent or guardian.
New MexicoComplete 50 hours of supervised driving practice, including 10 hours at nightProvisional drivers may not drive from midnight to 5 a.m. unless a licensed driver age 21 or older is in the vehicle, with limited exceptions for school, work, family, medical, or religious trips.Without a licensed driver age 21 or older, a provisional driver may have no more than one passenger under 21 who is not an immediate family member.
North Carolina60 hours, including 10 at nightAfter 9 p.m. until 5 a.m., a supervising driver is required.there may be one passenger that is under 21 that is not a member of the same household
North DakotaComplete and document 50 hours of supervised driving in a log, including night and varied road conditions.Not clearly published on the pages we checked.Not clearly published on the pages we checked.
OhioDriver must complete 50 hours of driving, with at least 10 hours of night driving, in addition to the driver education requirements.You may not operate a vehicle from midnight - 6 a.m. unless:A probationary driver may not carry more than one non-family passenger unless a parent or guardian is in the vehicle.
OklahomaService Oklahoma will require the parent to verify that the permit holder has completed fifty (50) hours of driving including a minimum of ten (10) hours of driving at nightNot clearly published on the pages we checked.The intermediate-license page says any passenger is allowed if a licensed driver at least 21 is sitting in the front seat.
OregonComplete 100 hours supervised driving experience, or 50 hours if you have taken and passed an approved Driver Ed courseYou cannot drive between midnight and 5:00 AM unless you are:For the first six months, a provisional driver cannot drive with a passenger under 20 who is not immediate family. For the second six months, the limit is three such passengers. The limits do not apply with a parent, stepparent, legal guardian, or a course instructor.
PennsylvaniaIf you are under the age of 18, you are required to complete at least 65 hours of behind-the-wheel skill-building including no less than 10 hours of nighttime driving and five (5) hours of bad weather driving before taking your Road Test.A junior license holder may not drive between 11 p.m. and 5 a.m. unless a parent, guardian, person in loco parentis, or spouse who is at least 18 is with them, with exceptions the manual lists for work, volunteer service, and school activities.A junior license holder may not carry more than one passenger under 18 who is not immediate family, unless a parent, guardian, person in loco parentis, or spouse age 18 or older is in the vehicle. After six months the manual raises that limit to three.
Rhode Islanda minimum of fifty (50) hours of experience with ten (10) of those at nightA limited provisional license holder may drive without supervision from 5:00 a.m. to 1:00 a.m., and also when driving to or from work or listed volunteer emergency duties. Supervised driving is allowed at any time.one passenger younger than twenty-one (21) years of age is allowed in the vehicle.
South CarolinaYou have practiced driving with a qualified, licensed individual for at least 40 hours, including ten hours of night driving.You may drive alone from 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. (8 p.m. during daylight savings time).A conditional or special restricted driver cannot have more than two passengers under 21 unless a licensed adult who is at least 21 is in the car, with an exception for family members or students traveling to and from school.
South DakotaYou must have a minimum of 50 hours of parent/guardian supervised driving (including 10 hours at night and 10 hours in inclement weather) to upgrade to a Restricted Permit.On a restricted permit, the driver may drive alone, with parent or guardian permission, from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m., a parent or legal guardian must sit beside the driver except for the exceptions the page lists.Not clearly published on the pages we checked.
TennesseeMust have 50 hours of behind the wheel driving experience including 10 hours of night drivingAn intermediate restricted license holder may not drive between 11 p.m. and 6 a.m.An intermediate restricted license holder may have only one passenger.
Texas30 hours of behind the wheel driving practice, of which at least 10 hours must be at night with a licensed driver 21 years of age or older in the vehicle at all times.A provisional license holder may not drive between midnight and 5:00 a.m. except for work, school activities, or emergencies.A provisional license holder may not drive with more than one passenger under 21 who is not a family member.
Utah40 hours, including 10 after sunsetNo driving between the hours of midnight and 5 am unless accompanied by a licensed driverFor the first six months or until age 18, you cannot drive with non-immediate family members
Vermontforty hours of practice behind the wheel, with at least ten hours being nighttime drivingNot clearly published on the pages we checked.During the first three months of a junior license, the driver may drive alone or with a listed supervising person. With that person in the vehicle there is no passenger-count limit other than the number of safety belts. Immediate family members may be transported during the second three months. After six months there is no passenger restriction.
Virginia45 hours, including 15 after sunsetUnder 18: no driving from midnight to 4 a.m., with listed exceptions.you may carry only one passenger under age 21, unless accompanied by a licensed parent, or other adult acting in place of a parent, in the front passenger seat.
Washington40 hours in daylight and 10 hours at nightNo driving from 1:00 a.m. to 5:00 a.m. unless a licensed driver who is at least 25 is along. The night limit can end after one safe year.For the first six months, no passengers under 20 except immediate family.
West Virginiaa minimum of 50 hours behind the wheel, including the minimum of 10 hours at night or complete a driver's education coursebetween the hours of 5:00 am and 10:00 pmduring their first six months with any non-family members under the age of 20 as a passenger
WisconsinAccumulate a minimum of 50 hours of behind-the-wheel driving experience, 10 of which must be driving experience at night.From midnight to 5 a.m., a probationary driver may drive alone only between home, school, and work. Other trips need a parent, guardian, or another qualified licensed person in the front seat.A probationary driver may drive with immediate family and only one other passenger, or with a qualified adult.
WyomingYou have completed 50 actual driving hours behind the wheel of a vehicle, including 10 hours of night driving.An intermediate permit holder may legally drive only between 5 a.m. and 11 p.m., unless the restrictions do not apply because a licensed adult with full privileges is in the front seat.An intermediate permit holder may not drive with more than one passenger under 18 who is not an immediate family member.

Frequently asked questions

Does every state use the same teen curfew?

No. A night rule appears here only when that state publishes one. Otherwise the cell says it was not clearly published.

Where are the ages?

Permit and first license ages are on the permit age table and on each state page.
