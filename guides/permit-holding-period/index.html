Learner permit holding period by state

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

A learner permit age is not the same question as how long you must hold that permit before the next step. Most states will not let someone take the road test or move to the next license stage until the permit has been held for a minimum stretch of time. That figure is on the permit ages table only when the state names it separately from the age. This table lists the holding period on its own, only when the state's own licensing page publishes it.

Massachusetts, Illinois, Colorado, and New York are not yet covered because the official sites could not be quoted.

Frequently asked questions

What is a learner permit holding period?

It is the minimum stretch of time a state requires someone to hold a learner permit before taking the road test or moving to the next license stage. It is a different figure from the minimum age to get the permit in the first place.

Does the holding period end automatically once the time passes?

Not on its own. Most states still require a road test, a clean driving record, or completed practice hours during that period. Read the state's own permit guide for the full condition before you plan around a date.

Why does a state on this table say the holding period was not clearly published?

The official page we opened did not state a figure we could confirm. Check that state's licensing agency directly before you plan around a specific date.
