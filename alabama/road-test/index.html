Alabama road test requirements

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 20 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Alabama does not publish a list of automatic road-test fails in the current driver manual. The November 2024 Alabama Driver Manual tells you who may come, what the examiner checks on the car, and what papers to bring. It does not name mistakes that end the drive on the spot. Road test section, November 2024 manual (official site)

Quick answer

  • Automatic-fail list: not in the November 2024 manual's road-test section.
  • Who takes it: Stage II, and anyone 18 or older applying straight for a Stage III license.
  • A 16-year-old: must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
  • The car: safe operating condition, brake lights, both turn signals, and a horn. Bring insurance and registration.
  • Booking: online up to two weeks ahead at select locations. Walk-ins are welcome at all testing sites. No single public booking URL was on the page.

ALEA homeHandbook

What the examiner does before you drive

A driver license examiner gives the road skills test. Before it starts, the examiner inspects the vehicle for safe operating condition and for brake lights, left and right turn signals, and a horn. The applicant must present proof of insurance and vehicle registration. A rental vehicle may be used only if the applicant's name is on the rental agreement as an additional driver. If a vision restriction requires corrective lenses, the manual says those lenses must be worn for the road test. Vehicle check (official site)

Stage II, and the path that skips it

Under 18, Stage II is the restricted license. It requires a passing road skills exam, and the exam is based on the driver manual. Those applicants also need at least 6 months on a Stage I learner permit. At 17, Stage III waits until the restricted license has been held at least six months. People age 18 and older may apply immediately for a Stage III license, and ALEA still requires a passing score on the road skills exam. Who must pass the road skills exam (official site)

Appointments and walk-ins

ALEA says Stage II road-skill appointments can be scheduled online up to two weeks in advance, at select locations, and that walk-ins are welcome at all testing sites. The page does not print one statewide booking link, so this guide does not invent one. Start at the ALEA home page linked above. Scheduling note (official site)

Why this page does not list fail-on-the-spot mistakes

ALEA still hosts a separate PDF whose first line is "June 2016 Edition". In that file the words "Causes for immediate failure and termination of the road test" appear. June 2016 driver manual (official site) The November 2024 road-test section does not use that heading, and it does not list those mistakes. This page follows the newer booklet. If a later manual adds a critical-error list, that list belongs here only after it is quoted from the new file. Compare the wait before this exam on the holding-period guide, and the practice hours on the teen licensing guide.

Frequently asked questions

Does Alabama publish automatic road test fails?

Not in the road-test section of the November 2024 Alabama Driver Manual. That section covers the vehicle inspection, a parent or guardian for a 16-year-old, insurance, and registration.

What should I bring to the Alabama road test?

Proof of insurance and vehicle registration. The examiner checks brake lights, both turn signals, and the horn. A 16-year-old must bring a parent or guardian. Wear corrective lenses if your license restriction requires them. A rental car is allowed only when your name is on the agreement as an additional driver.

How do I schedule the Alabama road test?

ALEA says Stage II appointments can be scheduled online up to two weeks ahead at select locations, and walk-ins are welcome at all testing sites. A single public booking URL was not on the page checked 20 Nov 2026.
