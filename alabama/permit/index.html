Alabama learner permit requirements

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 20 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

In Alabama, a Stage I learner permit starts at age 15. ALEA graduated license page (official site) says the applicant must be age 15 or older. The old wording on this page, about school papers for a fifteen-year-old, is the motor-driven cycle list, not the permit age.

Quick answer

  • Minimum age: 15.
  • Who must ride along: a parent, legal guardian, or licensed driver age 21 or older in the front seat, or a licensed or certified instructor in the front seat.
  • Before Stage II, if under 18: at least 6 months on Stage I.
  • Practice: 50 hours behind the wheel, or a State Department of Education driver education completion form.
  • Fees on the same page: $5 exam fee and $36.25 for the Stage I license. No checks.

ALEA homeHandbook

Who sits in the front seat

A Stage I license lets a teen drive only with a parent, legal guardian, or a licensed driver who is age 21 or older in the front seat. A licensed or certified driving instructor in that same seat also qualifies. Stage I supervision (official site)

Six months, then the restricted license

Applicants under 18 must hold the Stage I license for at least 6 months before a Stage II license. Stage II also needs a parent, grandparent, or legal guardian's permission to drive without supervision, and a passing road skills exam. The holding-period table compares that wait with other states. Six-month Stage I rule (official site)

Fifty hours, or driver education

ALEA wants a verification form, signed by a parent, grandparent, legal guardian, or certified driving instructor, saying the applicant completed a minimum of 50 hours of behind-the-wheel practice. The alternative is a completion form from the State Department of Education for an approved driver education course. Night limits and the passenger cap are on the teen licensing page, not on the permit itself. Practice hours (official site)

What a 15-year-old brings

The document page separates a learner license at age 15 from a license at 16 or older. At 15, ALEA lists a certified U.S. birth certificate from the Bureau of Vital Statistics, and it says no photocopies. Also a Social Security card, acceptable proof of school enrollment or graduation, and two proofs of principal residence. The fee page has the dollar amounts: a $5 testing fee and $36.25 for the license. ALEA does not accept checks. Learner license at age 15 (official site)

The written exam comes first

Stage I requires a passing grade on the written examination. ALEA says that exam is based on the Alabama Driver Manual. The written test page is the short version. The November 2024 manual is the booklet the questions come from. Alabama Driver Manual, November 2024 (official site)

What to check next

  1. Open the graduated license page and confirm age 15 and the six-month wait still match.
  2. Confirm whether you will log 50 hours or bring the driver education form.
  3. Use the document list for a 15-year-old, not the motor-driven cycle list.

Frequently asked questions

How old must I be for a learner permit in Alabama?

ALEA says a Stage I learner permit requires the applicant to be age 15 or older.

How long do I hold an Alabama learner permit?

Applicants under 18 must hold the Stage I license for at least 6 months before applying for a Stage II license.

How many supervised hours does Alabama publish?

A signed form must verify a minimum of 50 hours of behind-the-wheel practice, unless the applicant submits a State Department of Education completion form for an approved driver education course.
