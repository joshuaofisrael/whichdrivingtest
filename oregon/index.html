Oregon driving test requirements and DMV guide

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Oregon. It is written from Oregon Driver and Motor Vehicle Services pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: Must be at least 15 years of age; and.
  • First license: Be 16 or 17 years old;.
  • Written test: Not clearly published on the pages we checked.
  • Road test: Not clearly published on the pages we checked.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Oregon driver license or permit?

A full document list was not confirmed on the pages checked 16 Nov 2026. Start with the DMV site linked here.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Oregon?

An instruction permit from another state or the District of Columbia counts toward the six months required before a provisional license.

How do I book the Oregon road test?

A public scheduling link was not confirmed on the pages we checked. Start at the DMV site linked here and follow their appointment steps.

Where are Oregon license and permit fees listed?

DMV publishes these amounts: REAL ID Class C instruction permit $60.00; DMV drive test $45. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
