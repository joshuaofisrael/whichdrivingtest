Oregon teen driver license rules

Teen licensing in Oregon is the graduated path published by Oregon Driver and Motor Vehicle Services. Adult applicants can have different rules.

Drivers 15, 16, and 17 years old get a permit called a Provisional Instruction Permit. Teen rules (official site)

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: Must be at least 15 years of age; and.
  • First license: Be 16 or 17 years old;.
  • Full license: until you are 18 or you have had your provisional license for 1 year.
  • Practice hours: Complete 100 hours supervised driving experience, or 50 hours if you have taken and passed an approved Driver Ed course.
  • Night rule: You cannot drive between midnight and 5:00 AM unless you are:.
  • Passengers: For the first six months, a provisional driver cannot drive with a passenger under 20 who is not immediate family. For the second six months, the limit is three such passengers. The limits do not apply with a parent, stepparent, legal guardian, or a course instructor.
