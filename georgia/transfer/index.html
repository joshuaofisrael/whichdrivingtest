Georgia license transfer rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

New residents must get a Georgia license within 30 days. A valid out-of-state license does not require a knowledge or road test. Transfer rules (official site)

If this summary does not say whether a knowledge test or a road test is waived, the pages we checked did not make that clear. Read the official page before you skip a test.

Learner permit from another state

Time on an out-of-state permit counts toward Georgia's one-year-and-one-day holding period, and the teen must still meet Georgia learner-permit rules. Permit from another state (official site)

Frequently asked questions

How do I transfer a license to Georgia?

New residents must get a Georgia license within 30 days. A valid out-of-state license does not require a knowledge or road test.

Does Georgia waive the tests for a new resident?

Only if the summary on this page says so. If it does not, the pages we checked did not make a waiver clear.

Can I drive in Georgia on a learner permit from another state?

Time on an out-of-state permit counts toward Georgia's one-year-and-one-day holding period, and the teen must still meet Georgia learner-permit rules.
