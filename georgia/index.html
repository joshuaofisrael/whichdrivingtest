Georgia driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Georgia. It is written from Georgia Department of Driver Services pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: You must be at least 15 years of age.
  • First license: You must be at least 16 years of age.
  • Written test: You must pass a vision exam and a knowledge exam.
  • Road test: You must pass a vision exam and road skills test.

Documents

  • Identity. One identity document, such as a certified birth certificate or U.S. passport. Source (official site)
  • Social Security number. The full Social Security number is entered on the license form and verified. Source (official site)
  • Georgia residency. Two residency documents, such as a recent utility or phone bill with a street address. Source (official site)
  • School enrollment. you must present a notarized Certificate of School Enrollment (DS-1) form from your school signed within the last 30 days Source (official site)
  • ADAP certificate. A Class D applicant must show Alcohol and Drug Awareness Program completion. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

New residents must get a Georgia license within 30 days. A valid out-of-state license does not require a knowledge or road test. Transfer rules (official site)

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Georgia driver license or permit?

DDS publishes a document list that includes Identity, Social Security number, Georgia residency, School enrollment, ADAP certificate. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Georgia?

Time on an out-of-state permit counts toward Georgia's one-year-and-one-day holding period, and the teen must still meet Georgia learner-permit rules.

How do I book the Georgia road test?

A public scheduling link was not confirmed on the pages we checked. Start at the DDS site linked here and follow their appointment steps.

Where are Georgia license and permit fees listed?

DDS publishes these amounts: Class CP instructional permit $10; Class D provisional license $10; Class C regular license (8-year term) $32. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
