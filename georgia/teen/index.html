Georgia teen driver license rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Teen licensing in Georgia is the graduated path published by Georgia Department of Driver Services. Adult applicants can have different rules.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: You must be at least 15 years of age.
  • First license: You must be at least 16 years of age.
  • Full license: Applicants must be at least 18 years of age.
  • Practice hours: 40 hours, including 6 at night.
  • Night rule: Class D: no driving from midnight to 5 a.m.
  • Passengers: During the second six months of a Class D license, only one passenger under 21 who is not immediate family may ride.

Practice hours

40 hours, including 6 at night Practice hours (official site)

Night driving

Class D: no driving from midnight to 5 a.m. Night rule (official site)

Passengers

During the second six months of a Class D license, only one passenger under 21 who is not immediate family may ride. Passenger rule (official site)

Holding period and full license

You must have held an Instructional Permit (Class CP) for a minimum of one year and a day. Holding period (official site)

Applicants must be at least 18 years of age. Full license age (official site)

Joshua's Law requires driver education before a Class D license, including 30 hours of classroom or virtual instruction. Teen rules (official site)

Class D drivers may not drive from midnight to 5 a.m. Teen rules (official site)

For the second six months of a Class D license, only one non-family passenger under 21 may ride along. Teen rules (official site)

A Class D applicant must already have held the learner permit for 12 months and one day. Teen rules (official site)
