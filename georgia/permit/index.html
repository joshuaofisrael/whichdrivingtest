Georgia learner permit requirements

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

In Georgia, Georgia Department of Driver Services publishes You must be at least 15 years of age. as the minimum age for a learner permit. Permit age (official site)

Quick answer

  • Minimum age: You must be at least 15 years of age.
  • Holding period: You must have held an Instructional Permit (Class CP) for a minimum of one year and a day.
  • Practice hours: 40 hours, including 6 at night.

Knowledge test

You must pass a vision exam and a knowledge exam. Written test (official site)
