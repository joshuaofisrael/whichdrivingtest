New Mexico teen driver license rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Teen licensing in New Mexico is the graduated path published by New Mexico Motor Vehicle Division. Adult applicants can have different rules.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: a teen must be at least 15 years old, and go into a MVD Field Office.
  • First license: a teen must be at least 15 1/2 years old and have successfully completed Stage 1.
  • Full license: full licensure at age 16 and six months.
  • Practice hours: Complete 50 hours of supervised driving practice, including 10 hours at night.
  • Night rule: Provisional drivers may not drive from midnight to 5 a.m. unless a licensed driver age 21 or older is in the vehicle, with limited exceptions for school, work, family, medical, or religious trips.
  • Passengers: Without a licensed driver age 21 or older, a provisional driver may have no more than one passenger under 21 who is not an immediate family member.
