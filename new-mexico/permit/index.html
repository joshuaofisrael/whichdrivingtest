New Mexico learner permit requirements

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

In New Mexico, New Mexico Motor Vehicle Division publishes a teen must be at least 15 years old, and go into a MVD Field Office as the minimum age for a learner permit. Permit age (official site)

Quick answer

  • Minimum age: a teen must be at least 15 years old, and go into a MVD Field Office.
  • Holding period: at least six months.
  • Practice hours: Complete 50 hours of supervised driving practice, including 10 hours at night.
