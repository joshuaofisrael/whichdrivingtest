New Mexico driving test requirements and DMV guide

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in New Mexico. It is written from New Mexico Motor Vehicle Division pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: a teen must be at least 15 years old, and go into a MVD Field Office.
  • First license: a teen must be at least 15 1/2 years old and have successfully completed Stage 1.
  • Written test: Pass the MVD Knowledge Exam. The exam may also be provided
  • Road test: Pass the MVD Road Skills Exam. If the teen has taken

Documents

  • Driver education enrollment or completion. A teen must show enrollment in, or completion of, a state-approved driver education program. Source (official site)
  • New Mexico residency. Two documents proving New Mexico residency are required. Source (official site)
  • Signed parent or guardian application. The teen and a parent or guardian complete and sign the permit application at a field office. Source (official site)
  • Identity and age. Permit applications require one proof of identity and age. Source (official site)
  • Identification number for REAL ID. A REAL ID permit also requires one proof of an identification number. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

After establishing New Mexico residency, a driver must surrender any other state's license and apply for a New Mexico license. A current out-of-state license waives the written and road tests. Drivers moving from another state also pay a one-time records-check fee. Transfer rules (official site)

More detail is on the transfer guide.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a New Mexico driver license or permit?

MVD publishes a document list that includes Driver education enrollment or completion, New Mexico residency, Signed parent or guardian application, Identity and age, Identification number for REAL ID. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in New Mexico?

The MVD pages checked on 16 Nov 2026 do not clearly say that a learner permit from another state is accepted. Confirm with MVD before you drive or apply.

How do I book the New Mexico road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through MVD, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are New Mexico license and permit fees listed?

MVD publishes these amounts: Instructional permit $10.00; Provisional license $18.00; Four-year driver license $18.00; Eight-year driver license $34.00. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
