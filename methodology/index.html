Sources and methods

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Sources we use

State pages use that state's official licensing agency only. The name differs. It may be a DMV, BMV, MVD, RMV, Department of Public Safety, Department of Driver Services, or a secretary of state. The federal REAL ID guide uses the Department of Homeland Security and TSA only for the federal rule, not for a state's filing steps.

Sources we do not use

Test prep sites, driving schools, insurance sites, and "we will file your DMV paperwork" businesses are not sources. If a figure is not on the official page, we leave it out.

Fees, ages, and documents

A dollar amount is copied only when the official page prints it. Ages and practice hours are included only when the official page states them. Document lists are shortened and linked. They are not a full legal extract of the handbook.

Dates

The checked date is the day we opened the official page. We do not refresh that date unless the page was opened again. Rules change, so the agency page wins.
