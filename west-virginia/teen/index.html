West Virginia teen driver license rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Teen licensing in West Virginia is the graduated path published by West Virginia Division of Motor Vehicles. Adult applicants can have different rules.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: To obtain a Level 1 GDL instruction permit, you must be at least 15 years old.
  • First license: Be at least 16 years of age.
  • Full license: be at least 17 years old, and have successfully completed all requirements.
  • Practice hours: a minimum of 50 hours behind the wheel, including the minimum of 10 hours at night or complete a driver's education course.
  • Night rule: between the hours of 5:00 am and 10:00 pm.
  • Passengers: during their first six months with any non-family members under the age of 20 as a passenger.
