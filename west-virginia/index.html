West Virginia driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in West Virginia. It is written from West Virginia Division of Motor Vehicles pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: To obtain a Level 1 GDL instruction permit, you must be at least 15 years old.
  • First license: Be at least 16 years of age.
  • Written test: you must be at least 15 years old, pass a vision screening, pass a knowledge test
  • Road test: Pass a road skills test (a maximum of three (3) attempts to complete)

Documents

  • Certified birth certificate. you must show a certified birth certificate issued by a state or governmental agency Source (official site)
  • Social Security card. The applicant must show a Social Security card signed by the applicant. Source (official site)
  • School Driver Eligibility Certificate. have a valid School Driver Eligibility Certificate from your county school board Source (official site)
  • Parent or guardian consent. A parent or legal guardian consents on form DMV-DS-23P, unless the applicant is married. Source (official site)
  • Identity and residency for a new applicant. New applicants and transfers provide identity, Social Security, and two proofs of West Virginia residency. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a West Virginia driver license or permit?

DMV publishes a document list that includes Certified birth certificate, Social Security card, School Driver Eligibility Certificate, Parent or guardian consent, Identity and residency for a new applicant. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in West Virginia?

The DMV pages checked on 16 Nov 2026 do not clearly say that a learner permit from another state is accepted. Confirm with DMV before you drive or apply.

How do I book the West Virginia road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through DMV, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are West Virginia license and permit fees listed?

DMV publishes these amounts: Level 1 instruction permit $7.50; Knowledge test, each attempt $7.50; Level 3 license at age 17 $2.50 per year. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
