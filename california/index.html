California driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in California. It is written from California Department of Motor Vehicles pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: You need to be 15 1/2 but under 18 years of age to apply for a learner's permit.
  • First license: Be at least 16 years old.
  • Written test: Take the knowledge test(s) . There are multiple choice questions based on the California Driver's Handbook , and a passing score is 80%.
  • Road test: Once you finish your practice hours and complete the 6-month waiting period , you'll take your driving test.

Documents

  • Identity and residency. Bring a California identification card or other proof of identity and residency. Source (official site)
  • Driver education certificate or enrollment. Have a Certificate of Completion/Enrollment of Driver Education Source (official site)
  • Parent or guardian at the visit. A parent or guardian must attend the office appointment. Source (official site)
  • Financial responsibility for the drive test. Proof of insurance that meets California requirements is required to take the driving test. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

A person who already has a valid license from another state applies for a California license at a DMV office and the listed steps include a knowledge test. The same page puts the driving test in the foreign-license steps, and it does not say the other-state drive test is waived. Transfer rules (official site)

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a California driver license or permit?

DMV publishes a document list that includes Identity and residency, Driver education certificate or enrollment, Parent or guardian at the visit, Financial responsibility for the drive test. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in California?

Have an instruction permit from California or another state for at least 6 months (or turn 18 years old) before scheduling your behind-the-wheel drive test.

How do I book the California road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through DMV, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are California license and permit fees listed?

DMV publishes these amounts: Class C original, renewal, or add a motorcycle license $46; Class C behind-the-wheel retest $9; Class C replacement, information change, or firefighter endorsement $37. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
