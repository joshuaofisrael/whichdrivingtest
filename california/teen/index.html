California teen driver license rules

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

Teen licensing in California is the graduated path published by California Department of Motor Vehicles. Adult applicants can have different rules.

You need to be 15 1/2 but under 18 years of age to apply for a learner's permit. Teen rules (official site)

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: You need to be 15 1/2 but under 18 years of age to apply for a learner's permit.
  • First license: Be at least 16 years old.
  • Practice hours: Practice driving for at least 50 hours with a California-licensed driver who is at least 25 years old. Ten hours must be at night.
  • Night rule: During the first 12 months of a provisional license, driving is not allowed between 11 p.m. and 5 a.m., with exceptions that require a signed note.
  • Passengers: With passengers under 20 years old, unless your parent or guardian or other California-licensed driver (at least 25 years old) rides with you.
