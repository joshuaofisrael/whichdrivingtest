Maine learner permit requirements

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

In Maine, Maine Bureau of Motor Vehicles publishes To obtain a permit, you must be at least 15 years old and need to complete a written driving exam as the minimum age for a learner permit. Permit age (official site)

Quick answer

  • Minimum age: To obtain a permit, you must be at least 15 years old and need to complete a written driving exam.
  • Holding period: at least six months.
  • Practice hours: complete 70 hours of practice driving (PDF) , which must include 10 hours of night driving.
