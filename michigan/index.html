Michigan driving test requirements and DMV guide

By Which Driving Test. Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Not a government site. Not affiliated with any DMV. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules and fees change. Check your state licensing agency before you apply. Read the disclaimer.

This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in Michigan. It is written from Michigan Department of State pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: Teens must be at least age 14 years and 9 months to obtain a Level 1 License.
  • First license: Level 2 License - an intermediate license that limits passengers and the unsupervised nighttime driving for teen drivers at least 16 years old.
  • Written test: Learn about driving in both the classroom (24 hours) and behind-the-wheel (six hours), and pass a written test.
  • Road test: Driving skills test must be passed before the Level 2 License can be issued.

Supervised drivingTeens are required to keep a driving log and complete a minimum of 50 hours of supervised driving (10 hours at nighttime) before they can take the driving skills test, which is required for the Level 2 License.

Documents

  • Social Security number. A valid Social Security number (or a letter of ineligibility) Source (official site)
  • Identity, legal presence, and residency. at least two documents establishing Michigan residency Source (official site)
  • Parent or guardian signature. A parent or guardian must sign the application. Source (official site)

This is a short reading of the official list, not the filing packet itself. Bring what the agency page names.

License from another state

A new resident with a valid, unexpired license from another U.S. state or territory or Canada can convert it at a Secretary of State office. The new-resident page says a written test is not required; a vision test and photo are. Transfer rules (official site)

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring for a Michigan driver license or permit?

Secretary of State publishes a document list that includes Social Security number, Identity, legal presence, and residency, Parent or guardian signature. Bring the originals they name. This page only shortens that list.

Can I use a learner permit from another state in Michigan?

The Secretary of State pages checked on 16 Nov 2026 do not clearly say that a learner permit from another state is accepted. Confirm with Secretary of State before you drive or apply.

How do I book the Michigan road test?

Use the official scheduling link on this page. Book through Secretary of State, not a third party site that charges to file for you.

Where are Michigan license and permit fees listed?

Secretary of State publishes these amounts: Standard first-time license $25; REAL ID correction, license $9; REAL ID correction, state ID $10. Fees change. Read the official fee page before you pay.
