Wyoming learner permit requirements

In Wyoming, Wyoming Department of Transportation publishes 15-16 years of age, but it can be issued to anyone above that age who is learning how to drive. as the minimum age for a learner permit. Permit age (official site)

Quick answer

  • Minimum age: 15-16 years of age, but it can be issued to anyone above that age who is learning how to drive.
  • Holding period: at least 10 days.
  • Practice hours: You have completed 50 actual driving hours behind the wheel of a vehicle, including 10 hours of night driving.
