This is a short guide to the driving test and license rules in South Dakota. It is written from South Dakota Department of Public Safety pages opened on 16 Nov 2026.

Quick answer

  • Learner permit: To be eligible to get your instruction permit, you must be 14 years of age.
  • First license: You must be 14 years old and have met the conditions of an Instruction Permit (see above).
  • Written test: You will then need to take a knowledge test or have successfully completed a state-approved driver education course* to get your permit.
  • Road test: You must pass a drive test at a driver exam station
